Our Services

Comprehensive machinery alignment solutions for optimal performance

Precision Alignment Solutions

At Eco Machinery Alignment, we offer a complete range of professional alignment services designed to maximize your equipment's performance, reduce maintenance costs, and minimize downtime. Our certified technicians use state-of-the-art laser technology to deliver accurate, reliable results every time.

🎯 Laser Shaft Alignment

Our flagship service uses advanced laser technology to achieve precise alignment of rotating machinery. Proper shaft alignment is critical for reducing vibration, extending bearing life, and maximizing energy efficiency.

Applications:

  • Motor and pump alignments
  • Gearbox alignments
  • Turbine and generator sets
  • Compressor alignments
  • Fan and blower systems
  • Coupling alignments

Benefits:

  • Reduce energy consumption by up to 10%
  • Extend bearing life by 3-5 times
  • Minimize vibration and noise levels
  • Prevent premature seal and coupling failures
  • Decrease maintenance costs significantly
  • Improve overall equipment reliability

📊 Vibration Analysis & Monitoring

Comprehensive vibration analysis helps identify potential problems before they lead to costly failures. Our technicians use sophisticated vibration analyzers to detect issues like imbalance, misalignment, bearing defects, and resonance.

What We Detect:

  • Machine imbalance conditions
  • Shaft misalignment (angular and parallel)
  • Bearing defects and wear patterns
  • Structural looseness
  • Resonance and natural frequency issues
  • Belt and pulley problems
  • Gear mesh irregularities

Our Process:

  • Initial baseline vibration measurements
  • Frequency spectrum analysis
  • Trend monitoring over time
  • Detailed diagnostic reporting
  • Corrective action recommendations
  • Post-repair verification testing

⚙️ Belt & Pulley Alignment

Proper belt and pulley alignment is essential for maximizing belt life, reducing energy loss, and preventing excessive vibration. Our laser alignment tools ensure precise parallel and angular alignment of your belt drive systems.

Services Include:

  • V-belt drive alignment
  • Serpentine belt system alignment
  • Timing belt alignment
  • Flat belt alignment
  • Multi-pulley system alignment
  • Sheave alignment verification

Results You'll See:

  • Extended belt life (up to 3x longer)
  • Reduced belt noise and vibration
  • Lower energy consumption
  • Decreased bearing wear
  • Minimized belt slippage
  • Improved power transmission efficiency

📐 Geometric & Machinery Alignment

Geometric alignment ensures that machine components are positioned correctly relative to each other and to established reference points. This is critical for machinery installation, equipment relocation, and precision manufacturing processes.

Services Offered:

  • Machine tool alignment
  • Straightness and flatness measurements
  • Bore alignment verification
  • Roll alignment for paper and steel mills
  • Turbine and generator centerline alignment
  • Foundation and baseplate leveling

Applications:

  • New equipment installation
  • Machine relocation projects
  • Production line setup
  • Quality control verification
  • Precision manufacturing operations
  • Equipment rebuild projects

⚖️ Dynamic Balancing

Dynamic balancing eliminates vibration caused by uneven mass distribution in rotating components. Our field balancing services can be performed on-site, minimizing downtime and transportation costs.

Equipment We Balance:

  • Fans and blowers (all sizes)
  • Pump impellers
  • Motor rotors and armatures
  • Drive shafts and spindles
  • Grinding wheels
  • Centrifuges
  • Turbine rotors

Benefits:

  • Eliminate vibration-related issues
  • Extend bearing and seal life
  • Reduce noise levels
  • Improve product quality
  • Prevent structural fatigue
  • Comply with industry standards
